# Candidates v3 — 200 new designs (2026-09-24)

Maintainer: a long candidate list is the point — "a candidate is cheap, a full
monster with all animations approved is expensive", and a candidate is removed
only when he knows he will never add it. Nothing here is a commitment to
animate; it is a bench to bring to life from at any time.

Rules these were written to (same as v2): no id or name already used by the
412 existing monsters/candidates/retired/graduated; names ≤ 12 characters;
prompts are silhouette-first (one shape a 32–48 px sprite still reads),
then colour, then two to four distinguishing features; sizes small (32–48 px),
standard (64–176), big (224–240); the land mix roughly follows the ground
histogram (grass 38 %, bog 24 %, grey stone 13 %, snow 10 %, lava, shore,
caves, ruins/houses); the Come (fallen animals), the Made (remembering that
pooled and got up) and the Hollowed (emptied, still smiling) are all present.
