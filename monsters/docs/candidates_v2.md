# Candidates v2 — 100 new designs (draft list, nothing generated)

Designed 2026-09-18 for the maintainer's review. NOTHING here is on PixelLab and no
generations have been spent. Ids and names are checked unique against the 57 shipped
monsters, the 100 v1 candidates and the 54 retired designs.

## Why these 100

- **The world is the brief.** Land is 38% grass, 24% bog, 13% grey stone, 10% snow;
  lava is 90 cells. v1 over-served lava, black rock and paving and under-served grass
  and bog. This set follows the ground histogram, with the Cave weighted above its
  cell share because nine named places are caves.
- **The ladder stops at 20.** All 96 tuned monsters sit in levels 1-20. This set runs
  2-50, most of it above 20, so the mountain's 46 levels have something on them.
- **The lore's three kinds carry the design**: the Come (fell, having lived), the Made
  (remembering that pooled with nothing to be), the Hollowed (emptied, kept the outside).
  Made 64, Hollowed 19, Come 17 — the Come are thin on purpose, the shipped 57 are
  almost all animals.
- **Silhouettes the model can draw eight ways.** No wingspans, no thin horns, no stilt
  legs: the retired list is full of them. Wings are folded or wrapped, snakes and worms
  are coiled, trees are squat, furniture is a box on legs.
- **No more mammals.** Of v1's baby mammals and quadrupeds he picked almost none, and
  the shipped roster is wolves, bears, cats and horses already.
